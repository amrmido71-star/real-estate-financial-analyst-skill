# Financial Metrics — Definitions & Formulas

## Profitability

### Revenue
- **Definition:** Recognized sales per accounting standard.
- **For Real Estate:** Recognized via POC or handover. Not equal to collections.
- **Formula:** Σ (Units delivered × POC) or handover value.

### Cost of Revenue (COR)
- **Includes:** Land amortization + Construction cost (POC) + Direct infrastructure
- **Excludes:** S&M, G&A, Financing (those are Opex / Below EBITDA)

### Gross Profit & Gross Margin
```
Gross Profit = Revenue − COR
Gross Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue × 100
```
- **Interpretation:** 30-45% healthy for residential (MENA). <20% = watchlist.

### Operating Profit
```
Operating Profit = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses (S&M + G&A)
```

### EBITDA
```
EBITDA = Operating Profit + Depreciation + Amortization
       = EBIT + D&A
       ≈ Cash operating profit before capex & financing
```
- Used for leverage: Net Debt / EBITDA

### EBIT
```
EBIT = EBITDA − D&A
```

### Net Profit & Net Margin
```
Net Profit = EBIT − Interest − Tax
Net Margin = Net Profit / Revenue × 100
```
- **Healthy:** >15% residential, >20% commercial

## Efficiency & Return

### ROI (Return on Investment)
```
ROI = (Gain − Cost) / Cost × 100
```
- Simple, non-time-weighted. Use for quick comparison, not for multi-year projects.

### ROE (Return on Equity)
```
ROE = Net Profit / Average Equity × 100
```

### ROIC
```
ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital × 100
NOPAT = EBIT × (1 − Tax Rate)
Invested Capital = Equity + Interest-bearing Debt
```

### Cash-on-Cash Return
```
Cash-on-Cash = Annual Pre-Tax Cash Flow / Equity Invested × 100
```
- Relevant for rental/annuity, also for development equity distributions per year.

### Equity Multiple
```
Equity Multiple = Total Distributions / Equity Invested
```
- >1.0 means profit; 2.0 = doubled equity. No time value — pair with IRR.

## Liquidity & Working Capital

### Working Capital Requirement
```
WCR = Receivables + Inventory (Unsold Units at Cost) − Payables
```
- High WCR = cash tied up; common in development (large inventory + receivables).

### Receivables Aging
- Buckets: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-180, 180+ days
- **Overdue Ratio** = Overdue (>90d) / Total Receivables
- >20% overdue = collection risk.

## Leverage

### DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)
```
DSCR = Net Operating Income (or Operating CF) / Debt Service (Principal + Interest)
```
- <1.2 warning, <1.0 critical (cannot service debt from operations)

### Debt-to-Equity
```
D/E = Total Interest-bearing Debt / Total Equity
```
- Development: <1.5 healthy, >2.5 critical.

## Formatting
- Always state period (Q1 2025, FY2024), currency, and whether audited/management accounts.
- Vertical analysis: each line as % of Revenue.
- Horizontal: Change vs prior = (Current − Prior)/|Prior|.
