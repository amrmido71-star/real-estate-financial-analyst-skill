# Real Estate Development Finance — Domain Knowledge

## 1. Development Lifecycle
```
1. Land Acquisition → 2. Planning & Design → 3. Approvals → 4. Pre-Sales & Marketing
→ 5. Construction → 6. Sales & Collections → 7. Delivery & Handover → 8. After-Sales & Facility Management
```
Finance is involved in every stage: feasibility → funding → budgeting → cost control → revenue recognition → cash management → exit.

## 2. Revenue Recognition (IFRS 15 / POC)
- Real estate sales typically use **Percentage of Completion (POC)** or **Completed Contract** or **Point in Time (handover)** depending on standard & contract.
- Distinguish three layers:
  - **Contracted Sales (Bookings)** = total contracts signed
  - **Recognized Revenue** = POC × Contracted (or handover-based)
  - **Collected Cash** = cash received per collection schedule
- These three diverge — analyze gaps: Sales > Revenue = unrecognized backlog; Revenue > Collected = receivables risk.

## 3. Cost Structure (GDC Breakdown)
| Category | Typical % of GDC | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|-------|
| Land | 15-30% | Often largest single item; acquired early |
| Construction (Hard Costs) | 40-65% | BOQ-driven, phased |
| Infrastructure | 5-10% | Roads, utilities |
| Soft Costs (Design, Consultancy, PM) | 5-8% | |
| Sales & Marketing | 2-5% of GDV | Commission 2-3%, marketing 1.5-2% |
| Financing Costs | 5-12% | Interest, fees |
| Admin / G&A allocation | 2-4% | |
| Contingency | 5-7% of hard costs | Critical buffer |
| Taxes, Fees, Permits | 1-3% | |

## 4. Sales & Collection Dynamics
- **Sales Velocity** = units sold / month; drives cash inflow timing
- **Collection Schedule**: typical 10% booking, 10% contract, quarterly installments, 10-15% on delivery
- **Aging**: monitor >90 day overdue — leading indicator of default/cancellation
- **Cancellation Rate**: >5% is warning (product, pricing, or financing issue)

## 5. Financing Structures
- Equity, Pre-sales (customer funding), Bank debt (construction loan), Mezzanine, Sukuk/Bonds
- **Peak Funding Requirement** determines facility size needed
- **DSCR** = Net Operating Cash / Debt Service — must stay >1.2-1.5
- Interest often capitalized during construction (part of GDC)

## 6. Key Documents Finance Reads
- Feasibility Study, Appraisal Report, BOQ, Cost Report, IPC (Interim Payment Certificate), Sales Register, Collection Report, Aging Report, Trial Balance, General Ledger, Budget, Cash Flow Forecast, Facility Agreement

## 7. Common Pitfalls
- Confusing contracted sales with revenue
- Underestimating contingency
- Ignoring collection delays in cash forecast
- Using IRR without checking NPV/hurdle
- Not stress-testing price −10% scenario
- Forgetting financing cost in GDC

## 8. MENA Specifics
- Markets: Egypt (EGP, off-plan dominant), KSA (SAR, Vision 2030), UAE (AED, escrow regulated — RERA)
- Escrow: UAE/KSA collections go to escrow — impacts free cash timing
- Inflation & FX volatility (especially EGP) — sensitivity must include 10-15% cost overrun case
